BUILT AROUND THE CHANGE THAT CAN DIVERT A PAYMENT

Each write has to earn its way through.

01

Verify the caller

Authenticate the service or Finance user. Check current status, role, and credential scope on every request.

02

Restrict the vendor

Authorize each identity for specific vendors and actions. Broad sync access cannot change bank details.

03

Require a second person

A separate Finance approver reviews the precise bank details submitted by the requester.

04

Forward only the approved values

Encrypt held details, compare the approval against the request, and write only after an exact match.

ONE CONTROLLED PATH

Hold. Verify. Apply.

Unverified requests remain held or blocked. An approval cannot change the submitted values, and the requester cannot approve their own change.
